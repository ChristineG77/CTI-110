## Steps to make a sandwich
- Go to the kitchen
- Open the cabinet next to the fridge
- Grab a flat plate
- Put the flat plate down onto the countertop by the fridge
- Close the cabinet next to the fridge
- Go to the table
- Take the bread package off the table
- Unscrew the tie on the bread package
- Take two slices of bread out of the package
- Put the slices of bread onto the flat plate on the countertop by the fridge
- Re-twist the bread tie onto the bread package
- Put the package of bread onto the table
- Open the kitchen drawer by the fridge
- Take out a butterknife
- Put the butterknife down onto the countertop by the flat plate with the bread
- Close the kitchen drawer by the fridge
- Open the cabinet above the stove
- Take out the peanut butter jar
- Close the cabinet above the stove
- Open the peanut butter jar
- Pick up the butterknife from the countertop by the flat plate with the bread
- Scoop out enough peanut butter to cover one face of the slice of bread on the flat plate with the butterknife
- Spread the peanut butter onto one of the slices of bread on the flat plate on the countertop without picking up the bread
- Put the knife into the sink 
- Take the slice of bread without peanut butter and put it ontop of the slice of bread with peanut butter on it, making sure the slice faces line up
