# The Sandwich Lab

## Steps to make a sandwich

## Assumptions

Standing outside of the kitchen
Bread is on the table
Bread is in a bag with a twist tie
Peanut butter is in the cabinet above the stove
Plates are in the cabinet next to the fridge above the countertop
Cutlery is in the drawer nearest the fridge
There is a sink
There is a fridge next to the countertop and the cabinets
There is a kitchen drawer next to the fridge
There is a stove
There are cabinets above the stove

## Steps
1. Go to the kitchen
2. Open the cabinet next to the fridge
3. Grab a flat plate
4. Put the flat plate down onto the countertop by the fridge
5. Close the cabinet next to the fridge
6. Go to the table
7. Take the bread package off the table
8. Unscrew the tie on the bread package
9. Take two slices of bread out of the package
10. Put the slices of bread onto the flat plate on the countertop by the fridge
11. Re-twist the bread tie onto the bread package
12. Put the package of bread onto the table
13. Open the kitchen drawer by the fridge
14. Take out a butterknife
15. Put the butterknife down onto the countertop by the flat plate with the bread
16. Close the kitchen drawer by the fridge
17. Open the cabinet above the stove
18. Take out the peanut butter jar
19. Close the cabinet above the stove
20. Unscrew the peanut butter jar lid
21. Put the peanut butter jar lid onto the countertop by the flat plate
22. Pick up the butterknife from the countertop by the flat plate with the bread
23. Scoop out enough peanut butter to cover one face of the slice of bread on the flat plate with the butterknife
24. Pick up one of the slices of bread from the flat plate
25. Spread the peanut butter onto one of the slices of bread on the flat plate on the countertop without picking up the bread
26. Put the piece of bread with peanut butter onto the flat plate, making sure the face with the peanut butter is not facing the flat plate
27. Put the knife into the sink
28. Take the slice of bread without peanut butter and put it ontop of the slice of bread with peanut butter on it, making sure the slice faces line up
29. Put the sandwich onto the flat plate ontop of the countertop
30. Pick up the peanut butter jar lid from the countertop by the flat plate
31. Screw the peanut butter jar lid back onto thr peanut butter jar
32. Open the cabinet above the stove
33. Put the peanut butter jar back into its original place
34. Close the cabinet above the stove

## Down When
- Both slices of bread have had their faces connected with one of the faces that are touching being covered in peanut butter while sitting on the plate, the knife is in the sink and the peanut butter is but away
